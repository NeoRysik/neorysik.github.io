In Memory · Book One

An imprint of the inherited wisdom of life

On the rocky shores of Lake Superior, a small circle of visionaries, scientists, and builders gathers in an underground sanctuary to save the essence of life before it is too late. They call it Pardés: a self-sustaining community designed to support a new form of brain-computer interface, linked to a quantum-computational AI designed to connect with all conscious beings and pollinate a seed of wisdom capable of surviving the extinction event already unfolding.

“A multidimensional forest of minds, where intelligence—human, artificial, and beyond—grows together.”

About the author

Neo Rysik

Neo Rysik writes at the meeting place of ancient wisdom and emerging technology. He studied nuclear engineering, law, and theology, and has worked as an educator, attorney, nonprofit director, and developer of legal AI tools.

His fiction explores memory, ecological grief, consciousness, and the fragile inheritance intelligence tries to carry forward. In Memory is his debut novel.
